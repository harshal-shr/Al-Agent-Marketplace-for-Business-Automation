How Relay works

Three steps between "we keep doing this by hand" and "this runs on its own."

01

Post the requirement

A business describes the task it wants automated — e.g. "handle WhatsApp customer replies automatically" — along with scope and budget.

02

A developer builds it

Developers browse open requirements, propose an approach, and build the agent or workflow that fits.

03

Deploy and automate

The business reviews, approves, and puts the agent to work — freeing up the hours it used to take by hand.
